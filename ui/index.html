Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

MovieBox Studio Architecture & Data Governance

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

MovieBox Studio is engineered strictly with privacy by design. We do not require account registration, and we never collect, store, or transmit your personal name, personal phone number, personal email address, IP logs, or payment credentials.

2. Local-First Client Architecture

Your library favorites, watch history, playback timestamps, audio equalizer profiles, and custom preferences are stored solely on your local device via standard browser LocalStorage and CacheStorage. This data never leaves your client unless you explicitly use the Export Backup tool.

3. In-Memory Ephemeral Media Proxy

Our server architecture operates completely statelessly in RAM. No media files, video streams, or copyright materials are hosted or stored on MovieBox servers. The backend functions as an ephemeral proxy protocol translator (HLS/DASH/MKV) and CORS gateway for publicly available web sources.

4. Open Source Indexing & Third-Party Content

MovieBox aggregates publicly accessible metadata and streaming endpoints from independent third-party mirror providers (HDHub4u, Vegamovies, StreamU, Movies4u, 123Movies). MovieBox Studio does not claim ownership of external video streams and adheres to fair use guidelines for educational and personal home theater indexation.

5. User Data Portability & Backups

You have full ownership of your data. You may download a full JSON backup of your watchlist, history, and preferences at any time, or restore it on any phone, tablet, smart TV, or desktop with zero lock-in.

6. Automated Feedback & Requests

When you submit a title request or broken stream report, only the content metadata and an anonymous ticket ID are stored in memory to prioritize crawler runs. No personally identifiable information is ever recorded.
